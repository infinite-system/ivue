# Article-art opportunities — curation ledger

Which public posts earn a free-form illustration, the prompt to render,
and where it belongs. Produced by a full-archive scan (2026-08-22).
Cross entries off (~~strikethrough~~) as they ship; keep prompts updated
if a post's structure changes. House style rides every prompt: deep
navy background, glowing cyan/indigo accents, wide 3:2, no text in the
image. Pipeline: `.claude/skills/article-art/SKILL.md`.

## Selected

### the-object-graph-they-took ✅ SHIPPED (art-1)
- **Central metaphor:** A confiscated object graph coming home — `workspaceSet.active.editor.selectLine(42)` as a chain of hops the ecosystem broke into flat stores and IDs.
- **Prompt:** A deep navy void holding a constellation of luminous cyan nodes joined by taut indigo filaments into a branching graph; on the left half the filaments are severed and the nodes drift apart into a flat scattered plane of dim isolated points, on the right half the same nodes are re-linked into a single coherent structure glowing brighter along the connected path. Cinematic depth of field, volumetric haze, thin light trails tracing the path from root to leaf. Wide 3:2, no text, no letters, no UI.
- **Where:** Between `## The flight` and `## The homecoming` — right after the "anemic domain model" paragraph, so the image lands as the visual pivot from scattering to reconnection.

### circular-imports-dissolved
- **Central metaphor:** The cycle was never the problem — the clock was. Move every cross-module read from load time to first access and the same loop is harmless.
- **Prompt:** A deep navy scene with two overlapping rings of glowing cyan light locked in a mutual loop, and behind them a large ghostly indigo clock face dissolving into particles; the rings remain intact and calmly lit while the clock's hands break apart and drift away. Soft bloom on the ring intersections, fine grain, volumetric depth. Wide 3:2, no text, no numerals, no clock digits.
- **Where:** Immediately under `## The reduction: it was never the cycle — it was the clock`, before the idiom code.

### reactivity-is-an-allocator
- **Central metaphor:** Reactivity as a government above an imperative machine room — the OS floor stays plain and synchronous while the reactive graph above decides what exists.
- **Prompt:** A two-tier underground machine room in deep navy: the lower level is a dim, heavy, mechanical hall of pipes, valves and iron housings rendered in cold grey-blue with no glow; suspended above it hangs a weightless lattice of cyan and indigo light nodes, and thin luminous threads drop from the lattice into individual machines, a few of which are lit and running while the rest sit dark. Dramatic side lighting, volumetric dust, wide 3:2, no text, no signage.
- **Where:** Directly after the blockquote "Reactivity is not the plumbing here. It is the government." in `## The inversion: the OS layer is not reactive`.

### the-field-not-the-rules
- **Central metaphor:** Not policing a flat dangerous board with rules, but curving the field so degenerate moves have nowhere to stand.
- **Prompt:** A vast dark navy plane rendered as a glowing cyan grid, warped into a deep smooth curvature so that everything placed on it rolls inevitably toward one bright convergence point; a handful of small geometric pieces slide along the curve's natural channels while a few angular shapes find no purchase on the steep walls and slip away into darkness. Gravitational, physics-like, indigo rim light, wide 3:2, no text, no game pieces with symbols.
- **Where:** After the "A convention says 'please don't.' A field says 'there is no such move.'" blockquote, before `## The inversion table`.

### twenty-million-cells

- **Prompt:** An enormous flat lattice of tiny square cells receding to the horizon in near-black navy, almost all of them unlit and barely perceptible; a small rectangular window near the center burns bright cyan, its cells fully rendered and glowing, with a soft indigo falloff at its edges where cells fade back to nothing. Extreme scale contrast, subtle atmospheric haze, wide 3:2, no text, no numbers, no spreadsheet UI.
- **Where:** Right after the blockquote "Everything costs proportional to what's observed; nothing costs proportional to what exists." and before the columnar-typed-arrays paragraph.

Deep navy void around them, faint organic growth striations in the crystal, symmetrical composition. Wide 3:2, no text, no diagram lines.

### agents-built-an-editor
- **Central metaphor:** Gates instead of vigilance — a machine authoring at volume, with structural gates rather than care deciding what gets through.
- **Prompt:** A dark navy corridor where a vast stream of small glowing cyan fragments rushes forward toward the viewer and meets a series of tall, precise indigo gate frames; most fragments pass cleanly through the aligned apertures while a scattered few strike the frames and burst into dim sparks that fall away. Motion blur on the stream, crisp static gates, volumetric light shafts through each aperture. Wide 3:2, no text, no machinery labels.
- **Where:** Under `## Gates instead of vigilance`, after its opening paragraph and before `## Where the gate came from`.

## Skipped (and why)

- **rented-objects**, **organs-not-skeletons** — strong metaphors but very short posts with a hero + live demo; a mid-article image would crowd them.
- **one-kilobyte-feature**, **computed-is-a-cache**, **this-method-era**, **inheritance-exile**, **the-options-api-everyone-wanted**, **the-object-should-tell-the-truth** — short single-argument posts whose payload is code plus a live demo; art competes with the interactive component.
- **a-million-rows-twelve-divs**, **measured-not-promised** — the illustration IS the running benchmark; decorative art would undercut the measured-not-promised ethos.
- **introducing-ivue**, **introducing-invar**, **the-whole-story-in-small-words** — overview posts that inherit the deep posts' imagery; small-words is deliberately plain.
- **the-zeros-didnt-move**, **monolith-to-modular-in-a-day** — reportage built on greps and commit counts; the honest visual is tabular.
- **initialization-order-solved**, **most-linted-superpower**, **what-becomes-buildable**, **reactive-framework-for-the-ai-era** — enumerative structure; metaphors overlap posts already selected.
- **reactive-is-all-you-need** — the five-row reduction table does the work; a zoo image would be literal, not reinforcing.
- **the-test-is-a-subclass**, **vscode-hand-rolled-decade**, **patterns-the-author-never-wrote** — mechanism-heavy; their metaphors are already spent on selected posts.
