## Strategy notes

- Send RU channel pitches first because three channels expose named contacts and the Russian artifact can be reviewed natively before launch week.
- Send JSer.info Ping and Hatena Bookmark on launch day; they are link-discovery surfaces and do not require waiting for editorial acceptance.
- Submit Chinese editorial drafts only after removing launch language; SegmentFault, OSCHINA and InfoQ all describe original, useful, non-advertorial material as the acceptance boundary.
- Use one English artifact plus a one-paragraph local summary for Korea, Brazil, Vietnam, Poland and Spain; do not pay the translation cost until replies or comments prove demand.
- File open or next-cycle CFPs after the launch article has a public benchmark link; the talk abstract should point to a receipt that reviewers can run.
- Treat VueConf US, JSNation, Vue Fes Japan, JSConf JP, CascadiaJS and DevConf.CZ as next-cycle calendar items where the observed 2026 deadline is already closed.
- Pitch podcasts after the first two weeks of launch data; use the winning receipt as the episode hook and offer three questions rather than a product demo.
- For meetups, offer a 20-minute remote version first; it lowers organizer risk and leaves the longer Invar or signals talk for a later conference CFP.
- Never mass-bookmark, cross-post identical translations, or send a second pitch before the venue’s stated or inferred waiting period.
